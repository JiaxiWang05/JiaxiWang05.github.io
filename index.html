03 Publications

Publications

Submission AAAI 2027 Conference · 2026

EndoWAM: A Grounded World-Action Model for Generalizable Endoscopic Navigation

Jinsong Lin, Zikang Pan, Wanhao Liu, CHI KIT NG, Liangjing Shao, Yu Zihang, Ziyu Wang, Yin Wang, Jiaxi Wang, Jeremy Yuen-Chun Teoh, Zhiyong Xiong, Huxin Gao, Hongliang Ren

EndoWAM introduces a grounded world-action model for autonomous robotic endoscopic navigation, combining predictive visual dynamics, future target-region grounding, and action generation for robust navigation in deformable surgical environments.

  • Grounded World-Action Model
  • Predictive Visual Dynamics
  • Endoscopic Navigation
