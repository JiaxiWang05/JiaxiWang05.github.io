EndoWAM: A Grounded World-Action Model for Generalizable Endoscopic Navigation

Jinsong Lin, Zikang Pan, Wanhao Liu, CHI KIT NG, Liangjing Shao, Yu Zihang, Ziyu Wang, Yin Wang, Jiaxi Wang, Jeremy Yuen-Chun Teoh, Zhiyong Xiong, Huxin Gao, Hongliang Ren

A grounded world-action model for generalizable robotic endoscopic navigation — coupling predictive visual dynamics with action generation, and introducing future target-region grounding for robust, real-time control in deformable surgical environments.
