EndoWAM: A Grounded World-Action Model for Generalizable Endoscopic Navigation

Jinsong Lin, Zikang Pan, Wanhao Liu, CHI KIT NG, Liangjing Shao, Yu Zihang, Ziyu Wang, Yin Wang, Jiaxi Wang, Jeremy Yuen-Chun Teoh, Zhiyong Xiong, Huxin Gao, Hongliang Ren

EndoWAM is, to the authors' knowledge, the first world-action model for generalizable robotic endoscopic navigation. It introduces future grounding — predicting task-relevant target regions in future observations from intermediate denoising features of a video world model — and couples a lightweight diffusion transformer with a discrete action expert through a shared predictive representation, enabling real-time control in a single denoising pass. The work introduces EndoMotion, a robotic endoscopic motion dataset spanning ureteroscopy, esophagoscopy, and ERCP, and demonstrates strong zero-shot generalization to unseen viewpoints, environments, and targets.
